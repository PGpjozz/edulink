# BrightCampus Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

**Version 2026-01 — Annexure B to the School Service Agreement**

This policy applies to every person who accesses BrightCampus under a school's tenant — school
owners, principals, admins, HODs, teachers, learners and parents/guardians ("**Users**"). The
School is responsible for ensuring its Users comply.

---

## 1. Accounts and access

- Accounts are personal. Do not share sign-in credentials or let anyone else use your account.
- Use a strong password and keep it confidential. Staff must not store passwords in shared
  documents.
- Only access data you are authorised to see for your role. Attempting to access another school's
  data, another user's account, or data beyond your role is prohibited and is logged.
- The School must deactivate accounts promptly when staff leave or learners exit the school.

## 2. Lawful and appropriate use

Users must **not** use the Platform to:

- upload or send unlawful, defamatory, harassing, discriminatory or sexually explicit content;
- bully, intimidate or harass any learner, parent or staff member (including via the messaging
  features);
- upload content that infringes copyright or other rights;
- misrepresent academic records, attendance or financial information;
- send spam or unsolicited marketing to parents or staff;
- process personal information for purposes unrelated to school administration.

## 3. Protection of learner data

- Learner information (including SA ID numbers, grades, behaviour records and health-related notes)
  is confidential. Do not export, screenshot, or share it outside authorised school processes.
- Staff must follow the School's POPIA policies and the Data Processing Agreement.
- Report any suspected data breach or account compromise to the School's information officer and
  BrightCampus support immediately.

## 4. System integrity

Users must **not**:

- probe, scan or test the vulnerability of the Platform, or bypass authentication or rate limits;
- introduce malware or run automated scripts/bots against the Platform without written permission;
- reverse engineer, copy or resell the Platform;
- overload the Platform (e.g. bulk scraping).

Security researchers may report vulnerabilities responsibly to `____________________`.

## 5. Payments

- Fee payments are processed via PayFast. Never ask parents to share card details over the
  Platform's messaging features.
- Staff must not record fictitious payments or manipulate billing records.

## 6. Consequences of breach

- The School may suspend or remove a User's access for breach of this policy.
- BrightCampus may suspend accounts involved in security abuse, unlawful content, or attempts to
  breach tenant isolation, with notice to the School.
- Serious breaches may be reported to the school governing body, the Information Regulator, or law
  enforcement where required.

## 7. Changes

BrightCampus may update this policy with at least 30 days notice to the School. Continued use after
the effective date constitutes acceptance.
