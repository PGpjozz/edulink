# BrightCampus School Service Agreement

   at `brightcampus.co.za`;

   Physical address: `____________________`
   Email: `____________________`

2. **The School** identified in the attached Onboarding Order Form (the "**School**").

Together the "**Parties**".

---

## 1. Definitions

- **"Platform"** — the BrightCampus web application, including dashboards for provider, school
  owner, principal, school admin, HOD, teacher, learner and parent roles, and all related APIs,
  mobile/PWA access, and documentation.
- **"Order Form"** — the BrightCampus Onboarding Order Form signed by both Parties, recording the
  School's details, selected Plan, fees and go-live date.
- **"Plan"** — the subscription tier selected on the Order Form (Small, Medium or Large), including
  its learner limit, monthly fee, overage rate and feature set.
- **"Users"** — the School's staff, learners and parents/guardians who access the Platform under
  the School's tenant.
- **"School Data"** — all data submitted to the Platform by or on behalf of the School, including
  learner and parent personal information, academic records, attendance, messages and financial
  records.
- **"POPIA"** — the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (South Africa).
- **"DPA"** — the Data Processing Agreement between the Parties, which forms part of this Agreement.

## 2. Services

2.1 The Provider grants the School a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to access and use the
Platform for the School's internal educational administration during the Term, per the selected
Plan.

2.2 The Platform is provided as software-as-a-service. The Provider hosts, maintains and updates
the Platform and may improve or modify features, provided core functionality of the selected Plan
is not materially reduced.

2.3 The service levels in **Annexure A (Service Level Agreement)** apply.

2.4 Use of the Platform by all Users is subject to **Annexure B (Acceptable Use Policy)**.

## 3. Term and renewal

3.1 This Agreement commences on the **Effective Date** stated on the Order Form and continues for
an initial period of **12 months** ("**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier in accordance
with clause 12.

3.2 After the Initial Term, the Agreement renews automatically on a **month-to-month** basis unless
either Party gives at least **one (1) calendar month** written notice of non-renewal.

## 4. Fees and payment

4.1 The School shall pay the monthly subscription fee stated on the Order Form. Unless a custom fee
is recorded on the Order Form, the standard fees are:

| Plan | Monthly fee (excl. VAT) | Learner limit | Overage per additional learner |
|------|------------------------|---------------|-------------------------------|
| Small | R2,500 | 200 | R15 |
| Medium | R5,500 | 500 | R12 |
| Large | R12,000 | Unlimited | n/a |

4.2 Fees are billed **monthly in advance** and collected via **PayFast** or EFT as agreed on the
Order Form. Overage charges (learners above the Plan limit) are billed in arrears with the next
invoice.

4.3 Invoices are payable within **7 days** of issue. Late amounts accrue interest at the prime
overdraft rate published by the Provider's bankers plus 2% per annum, calculated daily.

4.4 If any invoice remains unpaid **14 days** after its due date, the Provider may, after written
notice, suspend the School's access until payment is received. Suspension does not relieve the
School of its payment obligations.

4.5 The Provider may adjust standard fees **once per calendar year** with at least **60 days**
written notice. Fee changes take effect no earlier than the end of the Initial Term.

4.6 All fees exclude VAT, which will be added where applicable.

## 5. School responsibilities

The School shall:

(a) ensure the accuracy of School Data it loads onto the Platform, including learner South African
ID numbers used for sign-in;

(b) appoint at least one Principal or School Owner user responsible for administering the School's
tenant, managing invites and deactivating leavers;

(c) obtain all consents and provide all notices required under POPIA and applicable education
regulations before loading learner and parent personal information (see the DPA and the Parent &
Learner Consent Notice template);

(d) keep account credentials confidential and notify the Provider promptly of any suspected
unauthorised access;

(e) use the Platform only for lawful school administration purposes and procure that its Users
comply with the Acceptable Use Policy;

(f) remain solely responsible for its own fee collection decisions, academic decisions, and
communications with parents made through the Platform.

## 6. Provider responsibilities

The Provider shall:

(a) make the Platform available in accordance with the SLA;

(b) process School Data only as instructed by the School and in accordance with the DPA and POPIA;

(c) implement and maintain the technical and organisational security measures described in the DPA,
including role-based access scoped per school, password hashing, and audit logging of sensitive
actions;

(d) provide support as described in the SLA;

(e) not sell School Data or use it for advertising.

## 7. Data ownership and export

7.1 As between the Parties, the School owns all School Data. The Provider owns the Platform, its
software, and all intellectual property in it.

7.2 Signed-in Users may export their own account data via the Platform's data export function. On
termination, the Provider will make a full export of School Data available in a commonly used
machine-readable format (e.g. CSV/JSON) for **30 days** after the effective date of termination,
after which School Data will be deleted or anonymised in accordance with the DPA, save for records
the Provider must retain by law.

## 8. Confidentiality

8.1 Each Party shall keep the other's confidential information secret and use it only for purposes
of this Agreement. Confidential information excludes information that is public through no breach,
independently developed, or lawfully received from a third party.

8.2 This clause survives termination for **5 years**; confidentiality of personal information
survives indefinitely.

## 9. Warranties

9.1 Each Party warrants that it has authority to enter into this Agreement.

9.2 The Provider warrants that the Platform will perform materially as described in its
documentation. Except as expressly stated, the Platform is provided "as is" and the Provider
disclaims all other warranties to the maximum extent permitted by law.

9.3 The Provider does not warrant that the Platform will be error-free or uninterrupted; remedies
for downtime are as set out in the SLA.

## 10. Limitation of liability

10.1 Neither Party excludes liability for fraud, gross negligence, death or personal injury, or any
liability that cannot be excluded by law (including the Consumer Protection Act, where applicable).

10.2 Subject to 10.1, neither Party is liable for indirect, consequential or special damages, loss
of profits, or loss of data (except loss of data caused by the Provider's failure to comply with
the DPA).

10.3 Subject to 10.1, each Party's total aggregate liability under this Agreement in any 12-month
period is limited to the **total fees paid by the School in that 12-month period**.

## 11. POPIA and data protection

11.1 The School is the **responsible party** for personal information of its learners, parents and
staff. The Provider acts as **operator** on the School's behalf.

11.2 The Parties shall comply with the **Data Processing Agreement**, which is incorporated into
this Agreement. In the event of conflict regarding personal information, the DPA prevails.

## 12. Termination

12.1 Either Party may terminate for material breach not remedied within **14 days** of written
notice.

12.2 Either Party may terminate immediately if the other Party is provisionally or finally
liquidated, sequestrated, or enters business rescue.

12.3 The School may terminate for convenience during the Initial Term on **2 calendar months**
written notice; fees for the notice period remain payable.

12.4 On termination: access ends, clause 7.2 (data export) applies, and accrued rights survive.

## 13. General

13.1 **Governing law:** South Africa. **Jurisdiction:** the Magistrates' Court having jurisdiction,
or the High Court where value requires.

13.2 **Notices:** in writing to the addresses/emails on the Order Form; email is valid notice.

13.3 **Entire agreement:** this Agreement (with the Order Form, DPA, SLA and AUP) is the whole
agreement and supersedes prior discussions. Amendments must be in writing signed by both Parties.

13.4 **Assignment:** neither Party may assign without the other's written consent, not to be
unreasonably withheld; the Provider may assign to a successor of its business on notice.

13.5 **Force majeure:** neither Party is liable for failure caused by events beyond reasonable
control (including national power or connectivity failures), provided the affected Party mitigates
and resumes performance as soon as practicable.
