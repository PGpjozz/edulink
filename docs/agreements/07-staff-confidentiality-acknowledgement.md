## I acknowledge and agree that:

1. **Personal account.** My BrightCampus account is personal to me. I will not share my password
   or allow anyone else to use my account, and I will choose a strong, unique password.

2. **Confidentiality.** Through the platform I will have access to personal information of
   learners, parents and colleagues — including learner ID numbers, academic results, attendance,
   behaviour records, messages and, depending on my role, financial information. I will:
   - use this information **only** for my duties at the School;
   - not disclose it to anyone not authorised to receive it;
   - not copy, export, photograph or screenshot it except as required for authorised school
     processes;
   - continue to keep it confidential after I leave the School.

3. **POPIA.** I understand the School is the responsible party under the Protection of Personal
   Information Act and that I must handle personal information in line with the School's POPIA
   policies. Most learners are children, whose information enjoys special protection.

4. **Acceptable use.** I have read and will comply with the BrightCampus Acceptable Use Policy,
   including the prohibitions on harassment, unauthorised access, and falsifying records.

5. **Accuracy.** I will take reasonable care to ensure the accuracy of marks, attendance and other
   records I capture.

6. **Incidents.** I will immediately report any suspected data breach, lost credentials, or
   suspicious account activity to the School's information officer.

7. **Monitoring.** I understand that sensitive actions on the platform are recorded in audit logs
   and may be reviewed.

8. **Departure.** When I leave the School or change roles, my access will be adjusted or revoked,
   and I will not attempt to access the platform thereafter.

Breach of this acknowledgement may lead to disciplinary action, revocation of access, and where
applicable, reporting to authorities.
