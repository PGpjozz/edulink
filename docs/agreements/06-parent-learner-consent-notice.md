# Parent & Learner Consent Notice — BrightCampus

**Template for the School to distribute — Version 2026-01**

> **How to use:** The School completes the blanks, prints or sends this notice to all
> parents/guardians before learner accounts are created on BrightCampus, and keeps the signed
> reply slips (or electronic acknowledgements) on file. Under POPIA, consent of a competent person
> (parent/guardian) is required for processing a child's personal information.

Dear Parent / Guardian,

## Introduction of the BrightCampus school management platform

**[School name]** ("the School") is introducing **BrightCampus**, a secure online school management
platform, to improve administration, communication and learner support. This notice explains what
information will be used and asks for your consent.

## What BrightCampus will be used for

- Learner enrolment records, class lists and timetables
- Marks, assessments, homework and report progress
- Attendance recording (with absence notifications)
- Messages between you and the School's staff
- School fee invoices and secure online payment (via PayFast)

## What personal information is processed

| Whose information | What is processed |
|-------------------|-------------------|
| Learner | Full name, South African ID number (used only for secure sign-in), grade and class, academic results, attendance, homework and behaviour records |
| Parent/guardian | Full name, email address, phone number, link to your child(ren), fee account and payment references |

## How the information is protected

- The School remains the "responsible party" under the Protection of Personal Information Act
  (POPIA); BrightCampus processes the information only on the School's instructions.
- Each school's data is isolated — only authorised staff of our School can see our learners' data.
- Access is role-based: teachers see only their classes; you see only your own child(ren).
- Passwords are securely hashed; sensitive actions are logged.
- Card and banking details are handled by PayFast, a registered South African payment provider —
  the platform never stores your card number.
- Information is **never sold** or used for advertising.

## Your rights

You may at any time:

- request access to, or correction of, your or your child's personal information;
- request deletion of information that is no longer required;
- download a copy of your own account data from the platform while signed in;
- lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa).

Contact the School's information officer: **[name]**, **[email]**, **[phone]**.

The full privacy policy is available at: **[platform URL]/privacy**
